# Climate Wavers Community Structure

## Purpose

Build a community that helps people receive useful hazard information, share local observations safely, and connect verified responders with affected communities. FlowOps Alerts is the alerting service; the community is where people learn, contribute, and coordinate preparedness.

## Recommended WhatsApp Community

**Community name:** Climate Wavers | FlowOps Alerts & Resilience

### 1. Announcements (admins only)

Purpose: official community notices, onboarding, preparedness guidance, and links to alert subscriptions. Keep discussion turned off so essential information stays easy to find.

### 2. Community Lounge

Purpose: introductions, questions, climate resilience conversations, and sharing community initiatives. This is the default discussion group.

### 3. Local Resilience Groups (add only when there is an active local lead)

Create one group per active county, town, or watershed, for example **Nairobi Resilience** or **Tana River Resilience**. Members share local observations, preparedness activities, and requests for help. Avoid creating many empty location groups.

### 4. Community Responders (restricted membership)

For approved community focal points, partner organizations, and response coordinators. Use for readiness coordination and referral to the FlowOps responder workflow. Do not treat membership as emergency-service accreditation.

### 5. Volunteer & Climate Action (optional)

For cleanups, restoration, training, and other non-urgent campaigns. Keep routine action planning separate from time-sensitive hazard updates.

## Keep the existing department groups internal

Rename or retain Operations, Business and Advisory, Technical Development, and Leadership as **Climate Wavers Team — [function]**. They are staff/working groups, not the public community structure. The public community needs clear entry points by member need, not by company department.

## How alerts and reports should work

- Subscribe to FlowOps Alerts through the platform for targeted, source-tagged alerts; WhatsApp Community messages should not be presented as a guaranteed emergency warning channel.
- Label information clearly: **Official source**, **Community report — unconfirmed**, or **Verified by [named role/source]**. Include time, location, source, and a link to the platform record when available.
- Send urgent, sensitive, or personally identifying reports through the platform's reporting flow or directly to an authorized responder. Do not ask members to post phone numbers, exact home locations, medical details, or images of affected people in open groups.
- Ask members not to forward unverified claims as confirmed fact and not to rely on AI summaries as a substitute for local emergency instructions.
- Route operational responder dispatch through approved organizations and the platform's responder workflow; community chat is not dispatch or incident command.

## Roles

- **Community lead:** owns purpose, onboarding, and monthly review.
- **Group admins/moderators:** welcome members, enforce conduct and privacy rules, pin verified guidance, and correct or remove harmful misinformation.
- **Local focal points:** collect consented observations and route reports; they do not confirm hazards unless explicitly trained and authorized.
- **Verified responder partners:** share official instructions and coordinate through the appropriate responder channel.

Start with two public groups (Announcements and Community Lounge) plus one restricted responder group. Add local groups only where a trusted focal point can moderate them. Review activity and member feedback after 60 days before expanding.

## First month rollout

1. Rename the community and write a one-sentence purpose in its description.
2. Pin the welcome message, privacy/conduct rules, and FlowOps Alerts subscription link.
3. Consolidate public discussion into Community Lounge; keep internal departments outside the public-facing community.
4. Appoint at least two admins and identify which members are authorized to verify or relay official information.
5. Invite existing members with a short explanation of what changed and where each kind of conversation belongs.
6. After 30 days, check whether people understand where alerts, reports, discussion, and responder coordination belong. Add local groups only where demand and moderation capacity exist.

## Suggested group settings and next steps

- Keep Announcements admin-only and pin the FlowOps Alerts subscription link and welcome message.
- Use Community Lounge for general conversation; avoid duplicating every alert there if people can subscribe through FlowOps Alerts.
- Keep Community Responders restricted to approved, identifiable focal points and partner organizations. State who approves members and who can verify information.
- Keep Climate Wavers Team only if it is genuinely internal. If all community members can see/join it, move internal planning to a separate private group.
- Keep each local group only while it has a named local moderator and useful local activity. Add new locations when a trusted moderator is ready.
- Consider renaming “Community Responders” to “Responder Partners & Focal Points” if membership includes volunteers who are not formally accredited responders.

### Recyclers & Recovery Partners

For recyclers, waste collectors, aggregators, and community recovery partners to share accepted materials, locations served, collection schedules, and partnership opportunities. Include material types, minimum quantities, operating area, and a contact method. Be clear about prices and conditions; do not post misleading recycling or environmental claims. Protect customer and worker information.

### Eco-Products Marketplace

For makers and vendors to showcase reusable, recycled, upcycled, and other lower-waste products, and for community members to ask questions or find suppliers. Each listing should include product details, materials, price, location, delivery or collection options, and a way to contact the seller. Sellers are responsible for accurate claims; buyers should confirm terms directly. Do not post payment credentials or sensitive personal information.

## Circular-economy group guidance

- Keep **Recyclers & Recovery Partners** focused on material recovery capacity and partnerships; use **Eco-Products Marketplace** for product listings and buyer inquiries.
- Invite providers to state their service area and accepted materials or product categories. Admins can verify identity and basic business details before labeling a provider as verified.
- Avoid presenting a WhatsApp post as a confirmed transaction, endorsement, or guarantee. Confirm price, quality, delivery, and payment terms directly between parties.
- If the group becomes busy, use the platform's directory or marketplace features for persistent listings and keep WhatsApp for discovery, questions, and community updates.
